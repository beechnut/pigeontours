{% capture summary %}
On our Italian Market Pigeon Tour, meet the pigeons of South Philly and the historic Italian Market on S 9th St.

Your guide will bring you around the neighborhood, visiting nearby flocks and nesting sites. You'll observe pigeons nesting above the market's unique shops, defying anti-pigeon spikes and netting, and boldly strolling through&nbsp;traffic.
{% endcapture %}

{% capture what_youll_do %}
- We start the tour outside a local cat café with a refresher on useful birdwatching skills. (Nothing says "cat energy" like watching birds.)
- Next, we'll visit an established flock along Washington Ave, and witness all types of pigeons and behaviors. Sometimes we even see a hawk or falcon chasing them!
- We visit a good pigeon nesting spot. If we get lucky (and we often do), we may see baby pigeons!
- We'll walk through the Italian Market, observing pigeons braving traffic and nesting in buildings. We talk about how pigeons got their modern reputation.
- We end the tour back at the cat café, where we talk about how to have a different relationship with our bird neighbors.
{% endcapture %}
