{% capture summary %}
On our London Pigeon Tour, meet the pigeons of Trafalgar Square and St. James’s Park!

Your guide will bring you around the historic London park — the site of the royal aviary of King James I — and share the fascinating story of London pigeons. Pigeons have served as wartime messengers, aristocratic status symbols, beloved fancy breeds (Charles Darwin bred pigeons!), and so much more.

You’ll visit nearby flocks and observe pigeons coexisting with the famous park waterbirds, defying anti-pigeon spikes and netting, and interacting with everyday Londoners, as they have for thousands of years.
{% endcapture %}

{% capture what_youll_do %}
- We’ll start the tour in Trafalgar Square and learn a little bit about the history of **pigeons in London**
- On our way to St. James’s Park, we’ll learn how to spot and identify **anti-pigeon architecture**
- We will visit **multiple pigeon flocks** in the park and have a chance to observe their behavior. We’ll learn about how they reproduce, navigate, and adapt to urban life
- We’ll meet **other types of birds**, from the famous park pelicans to grey herons, parakeets (fellow ferals!), greylag geese, coots, and many more, and learn how they fit into the **history of the park**
- The tour ends outside of St. James’s Café, where we’ll talk about the complicated **pigeon/human relationship** and their role as pests, pets, posties, and beyond.
{% endcapture %}
