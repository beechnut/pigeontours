{% capture summary %}
On our Center City Pigeon Tour, encounter Philly's pigeons from Independence Mall to City Hall!

Your guide will bring you through Center City, visiting pigeon flocks that live everywhere from grassy parks to nooks in buildings. You'll observe pigeons interacting with people and the built environment, navigating hostile architecture, and bathing in fountains.
{% endcapture %}

{% capture what_youll_do %}
- We'll start the tour at a historic park, where we'll teach useful birdwatching skills.
- Next, we'll visit a pigeon flock with unique coloring. We may occasionally see a red-tailed hawk on the hunt!
- We'll walk through Center City, encoutering smaller groups of pigeons looking for food or roosting.
- Lastly, we'll visit City Hall, where we'll discuss pigeon history and observe a larger flock snacking, drinking, and bathing.
{% endcapture %}
