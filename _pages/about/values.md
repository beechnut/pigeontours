# Our values

Our values guide how we approach our work at Philly Pigeon Tours.

We believe that acting in line with these values will move us closer to the world we want to&nbsp;create.

### Compassion

We strive to act with compassion towards all humans (including ourselves, one another, people on our tours, and people who dislike pigeons), as well as all other creatures (including pigeons and other&nbsp;wildlife).

We work to help others extend their compassion to more&nbsp;groups.

### Curiosity

We get curious about our own experiences and the experiences of&nbsp;others.

Our curiosity leads us to ask questions about pigeon behavior, human history and behavior, and our own thoughts and&nbsp;emotions.

We work to help others become more&nbsp;curious.

### Interconnectedness

We are in relationship with all things, including all communities of humans and the more-than-human&nbsp;world.

We take responsibility for the ways in which our actions impact the&nbsp;world.

We work to help others understand the ways in which all things are&nbsp;connected.

### Accessibility

We care about ensuring that everyone interested in learning about pigeons is able to access our tours and&nbsp;programs.

We strive to make our tour & program experiences great for all people, regardless of cultural background, race, religion, languages spoken, knowledge about the topics we cover, age, and&nbsp;[dis]abilities.

We work to normalize&nbsp;accessibility.
