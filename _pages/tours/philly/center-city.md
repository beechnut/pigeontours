{% capture summary %}
On our Center City Pigeon Tour, encounter Philly's pigeons around City Hall!

Your guide will bring you through Center City, visiting the pigeons that live around City Hall and Reading Terminal Market, two iconic Philly landmarks.
{% endcapture %}

{% capture what_youll_do %}
- We start the tour at City Hall with a refresher on useful birdwatching skills.
- Next, we visit a large nearby flock, observing and learning about pigeon behaviors.
- We visit Reading Terminal Market, where we'll observe a nesting site. If we're lucky, we'll see baby pigeons!
- To wrap up, we return to City Hall, the site of a hilarious story about a failed attempt at controlling pigeons. We'll discuss how pigeons got their modern reputation and what we can do about it.
{% endcapture %}
