{% capture summary %}
On a Center City Pigeon Tour, meet the pigeons of City Hall and Reading Terminal Market!
{% endcapture %}

{% capture what_youll_do %}
- We start the tour at City Hall with a refresher on useful birdwatching&nbsp;skills.
- Next, we visit a large nearby flock to observe and learn about pigeon&nbsp;behaviors.
- We visit Reading Terminal Market, where we'll observe a nesting site. If we're lucky, we'll see baby&nbsp;pigeons!
- To wrap up, we return to City Hall, the site of a hilarious story about a failed attempt to control pigeons. We'll discuss how pigeons got their modern reputation and what we can do about&nbsp;it.
{% endcapture %}
